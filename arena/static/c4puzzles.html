Connect Four puzzles live

1000 Connect Four positions with exactly one correct column, each asked as one decision, the way a Jev model is used: 250 each of win (one column wins at once), block (the opponent wins next turn unless blocked), fork (one move wins in two moves, the only winning move) and solver (the only move that keeps the result, by a perfect solver). No two puzzles are copies or mirrors of each other, and the correct column is spread evenly over the seven columns. Every puzzle is asked in three option orders, with the correct column at a different position each time, so a model's preference for a position cancels out.

Baselines

Random: the expected accuracy of a uniform guess (1 / legal columns; late positions have few columns left). Always centre: the legal column closest to the middle. Rule-based: wins if it can, blocks an immediate threat, avoids letting the opponent win on top, otherwise prefers central columns.
