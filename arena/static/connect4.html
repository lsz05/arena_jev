Connect Four

Two models play each other on a 7×6 board. Every opening is played once with each model moving first. A model sees the board from its own side (its discs are X) and chooses among the legal columns; the options say where the disc lands, with no hints about wins or blocks.

Points: win 1, draw 0.5, per game (the league ranking). TrueSkill: conservative rating μ − 3σ. P on block: the probability the model gave the blocking column(s) when it had to block, next to the chance level (the share of legal columns that block). Took win: share of the moves where a column would have won at once and the model played it. Blocked: share of the moves where the opponent threatened to win at once (and the model could not win itself) and the model blocked it. Let win on top: share of moves after which the opponent could win by playing on top of the new disc.
